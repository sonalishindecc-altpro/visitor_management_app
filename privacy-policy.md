# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: 15/08/2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **SecureGate – Smart Visitor Management System** ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About the Application

**SecureGate** is a smart visitor management application designed to help residential societies manage visitors and improve security.

The application provides features such as visitor registration, visitor check-in and check-out, QR/visitor pass scanning, resident notifications, visitor records, dashboards, and reports.

**App Name:** SecureGate – Smart Visitor Management System
**Developer/Organization:** SecureGate Team

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SecureGate may collect the following information:

### Account Information

* Name
* Email address
* Password/authentication information
* User role, such as Resident, Security, or Admin

### Visitor Information

* Visitor name
* Contact information, where provided
* Purpose of visit
* Visit date and time
* Check-in and check-out information
* Visitor pass or QR-related information

### Resident and Society Information

* Resident details
* Apartment/flat information
* Information required to associate visitors with residents

### User-Submitted Information

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide while using the App, including information submitted through account management or account deletion requests.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the collected information to:

* Create and manage user accounts
* Authenticate users
* Identify user roles and provide appropriate features
* Register and manage visitors
* Record visitor check-in and check-out
* Generate and verify visitor passes or QR codes
* Notify residents about visitor activities
* Maintain visitor records
* Provide dashboards and statistics
* Generate visitor and society reports
* Process account deletion requests
* Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App
* Improve the App and its features

## 4. Firebase and Data Storage

SecureGate uses **Firebase services**, including **Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore**, to support user authentication and application data storage.

Information stored through Firebase may include account information, resident information, visitor records, and other data required for the App's functionality.

Firebase processes data according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

## 5. QR Codes and Visitor Passes

SecureGate may use QR codes or digital visitor passes for visitor verification and check-in/check-out.

QR codes may contain or reference information required to identify and verify a visitor or visit. They are used only for the visitor management and security functionality of the App.

## 6. Notifications

SecureGate may send notifications to residents or other authorized users regarding visitor activities, visitor requests, security-related events, or important application updates.

Notification permissions may be managed through the user's device settings where supported.

## 7. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Information may be accessible to authorized users within the society management system, such as residents, security personnel, or administrators, where necessary for the App's functionality.

Information may also be processed by third-party service providers, such as Firebase, that help us operate and maintain the App.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of the App and its users.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage system or Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services, maintain visitor and security records, operate the system, or comply with applicable requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely removed where appropriate.

## 10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SecureGate account through the **Delete Account** facility provided by the App.

The account deletion request may require information such as the user's name, email address, or account details to verify the request.

After receiving a valid request, applicable account and associated information will be deleted or handled according to our data-retention requirements.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legitimate security, operational, or legal purposes.

## 11. Children's Privacy

SecureGate is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 12. Third-Party Services

SecureGate may use third-party services to provide certain application functionality, including:

* Firebase Authentication
* Firebase Cloud Firestore
* Other services required for application notifications or infrastructure

These third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our practices, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the **Last Updated** date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## 14. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the SecureGate project team through the contact information provided within the application.
